Haneen and Anhar, you are not just my friends — you are the two most precious people in my life. I made this website just so you would know how much I love you both.

Haneen, you are the youngest, and truly the most beautiful girl my eyes have ever seen. Anhar, you are just as beautiful, and our friendship has lasted for over 9 years — the best years of my life. Even though we were separated for 3 years, we never forgot each other. Anhar has been my friend since kindergarten and is the most caring person I know; even though she can get angry sometimes, she has the purest heart. Haneen is completely on my wavelength — we share the exact same mindset! Even though Anhar is a bit different from us, our bond never changes.

I always remember back during the national exams when Anhar got upset with us, and Haneen and I were complaining and ranting about it, but we still worked it out and came back even stronger as friends. I love those moments when we walked back together — Anhar would head home, but Haneen would come over to my house. Both of our families love all of us because we've never done anything wrong to make them dislike us.

I'm always the spontaneous one who loves to make everyone laugh, especially Anhar when she's not in the mood — Haneen and I always try our best to cheer her up so we can talk and laugh together. Remember when we even raised a cat together for just an hour before my parents put it outside?

Even though Haneen and Anhar live close to each other while my house is further away, I used to go visit so we could head to exams together. And when we reunited for the second time in the same class, even after I transferred to another school later on while you two stayed together, nothing came between us — I would still come visit you during break time.

At the end of the day, I just want to say: I love you guys so much!

— Asuma 💗
